### Unexplained by the script, classified here

These notes were not tagged `known difference`, but they are the documented LO implicit-intersection / bool-display behaviour, not Omacell bugs. Omacell matches the committed corpus (Excel-facing).

| Formula | Omacell | LibreOffice | Classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| `COLUMN(C5:E5)` | `{3,4,5}` | `3` | LO implicit intersection vs dynamic-array spill (known-differences: array-lifting) |
| `ASIN({0,0})` | `{0,0}` | `0` | same |
| `--TRUE` | `1` | `TRUE` | Excel bool arithmetic yields a number; LO CSV kept a logical |

The other 38 rows matched.
